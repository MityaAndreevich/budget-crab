Frequently asked questions

Does Budget Crab connect to my bank?

No. The app does not connect to any bank, payment network, or financial data aggregator. You enter transactions manually (typing or voice). This is intentional — your data stays on your iPhone and we never receive it.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, in the app's protected container, encrypted by iOS Data Protection. Your data stays on your iPhone. We never receive it. If you use iCloud Backup, your iPhone's backup can include some of it, such as your settings and the widget's summary; Apple encrypts it and we can't read it. The app keeps its transaction database out of the backup, so before moving to a new iPhone, export a CSV. Importing it on the new iPhone needs Premium or the free 14-day preview.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your transactions are removed with the app. Because nothing is stored in the cloud by us, deleting the app erases your local data. Export to CSV first if you want to keep a copy.

Does voice entry send my audio to a server?

No. Voice entry uses Apple's on-device Speech framework with the requiresOnDeviceRecognition setting enforced. Audio is processed in memory on your phone and is never transmitted.

Is voice entry available in my language?

Voice entry uses Apple's on-device Speech framework, so it works in the languages your iPhone has downloaded dictation for. Open iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation (and add the language under General → Language & Region) to install additional dictation packs. If the microphone button doesn't appear in Budget Crab, your iPhone doesn't have on-device speech recognition for that language.

How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new phone?

Open the app on the new phone. Go to Settings → Premium → "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed in to the same Apple ID that made the original purchase, your Premium status will be restored from Apple's records.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app → tap your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → Budget Crab Premium → Cancel Subscription. The app itself cannot cancel subscriptions; this is handled by Apple.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Data → Export to CSV. The file is generated on your phone and can be shared via any iOS share sheet target (Mail, Files, AirDrop, etc.).

Why don't all labels change immediately when I switch language?

Some text updates instantly. Others — like the dashboard summary — update the next time you open Budget Crab. This is normal. Close the app fully (swipe up from the home indicator) and reopen for the complete language change.

Will there be CloudKit sync between my iPhone and iPad?

It's on the roadmap as an opt-in feature that would use Apple's CloudKit in your own iCloud account. We would never receive your data. Today the app is iPhone-only and local-only.

I have feature suggestions. Where do I send them?

Email support@budgetcrab.app. Every suggestion is read by Dmitry. We prioritize based on how many people ask for the same thing.
