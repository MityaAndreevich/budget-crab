The architecture is the privacy promise.

Budget Crab stores everything on your iPhone. There are no servers to receive your data, no analytics SDKs, no advertising trackers.

Voice entry runs on-device using Apple's Speech framework. Your audio never leaves your phone.

No banks

No account linking. You type or speak your transactions. The app never asks for your bank credentials.

No cloud

No server-side sync. No backups to our infrastructure. Your data stays in the app's container on your device.

No account

No sign-up. No email required. Open the app, start tracking. Premium features are unlocked through Apple's StoreKit.

Voice + Siri

Say "Hey Siri, add ten dollars groceries" and the transaction lands locally. On-device speech recognition, never uploaded.
