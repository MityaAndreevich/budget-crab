Privacy Policy

How Budget Crab Money Tracker handles your information.

Effective date: July 1, 2026
Last updated: November 6, 2026
Applies to: Budget Crab Money Tracker for iOS, version 1.0 and later.
Updated November 6, 2026 Sections 2.1 and 5 now say exactly how iOS encrypts the app's data and what iCloud Backup can include. Nothing the app does has changed.
Short version Budget Crab stores everything on your iPhone. We do not run servers that receive your financial data. We do not use analytics. We do not use advertising SDKs. We do not collect or sell personal information.

1. Who we are

Budget Crab Money Tracker is an iOS personal finance application developed and operated independently by Dmitry Logachev. References to "we", "us", or "the app" in this policy mean Budget Crab Money Tracker.

2. What information the app handles

2.1 Information you enter

You can record financial transactions, categories, accounts, merchants, notes, and similar data. This information is stored locally in the iOS application container on your iPhone. It is encrypted by iOS Data Protection, which ties decryption to your device passcode. After you first unlock your iPhone following a restart, the app can open it in the background, for example when you add a transaction from the Shortcuts app.

2.2 Information the app does not collect

  • The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • The app does not access your contacts, location, photos, health data, browsing history, or search history outside the app.
  • The app does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) or vendor identifiers for tracking.
  • The app does not connect to your bank, financial institution, or any third-party financial data aggregator.
  • The app does not transmit your transactions, balances, categories, or notes to any server we operate.

2.3 On-device speech recognition

If you use the optional voice entry feature, your speech is processed on-device using Apple's Speech framework with the requiresOnDeviceRecognition setting enforced. Audio is not sent to Apple servers and is not transmitted off your device. Audio buffers exist only in memory during a single dictation and are never written to disk.

2.4 Optional file imports you initiate

If you import a CSV file through the system file picker, the file is processed locally on your iPhone. The app does not transmit imported files anywhere.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

The app offers optional Premium features through in-app purchases handled exclusively by Apple's StoreKit. When you purchase a subscription or a one-time purchase:

  • Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your card number, billing address, or other payment instrument data.
  • The app queries Apple's StoreKit to verify your active entitlements. This query happens on your device and uses Apple-managed infrastructure.
  • We do not log, store, export, or share your transaction identifiers, original transaction identifiers, trial status, or expiration dates anywhere off your device.

For information about Apple's handling of purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Analytics, crash reporting, and tracking

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or attribution SDK. We do not operate a custom analytics or crash reporting system of our own.

If you have opted in to Apple's "Share With App Developers" setting on your iPhone, Apple may share aggregated, anonymized crash and performance data with us through App Store Connect. This sharing is controlled by your iPhone settings, not by the app.

5. Backups and sync

Your data stays on your iPhone. We never receive it. If you use iCloud Backup, your iPhone's backup can include some of it, such as your settings and the widget's summary; Apple encrypts it and we can't read it. The app keeps its transaction database out of the backup, so before moving to a new iPhone, export a CSV. Importing it on the new iPhone needs Premium or the free 14-day preview. Today the app has no cloud sync or backup feature of its own.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 years of age and does not knowingly collect any information from children. The age rating in the App Store reflects this.

7. Your control over your data

  • You can delete individual transactions, categories, accounts, or merchants at any time from within the app.
  • You can erase all app data by deleting the app from your iPhone. Because all data is stored locally, deleting the app removes the data.
  • The app supports CSV export so you can carry your data out of the app at any time.
